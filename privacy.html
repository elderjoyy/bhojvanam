Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Annavan (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website or submit an enquiry or expression of interest. We aim to collect only information that is reasonably useful for responding to you and developing the Annavan community.

1. Information we collect

When you submit our website form, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, city or area, state, the category of your interest, the type of space or participation relevant to you, and any message you choose to provide. Please do not submit passwords, financial account details, identity-document numbers, health information or other sensitive personal information through the form.

Our hosting and website service providers may also process basic technical information, such as browser, device, IP address and request logs, to deliver, secure and maintain the website.

2. How we use information

We use the information you submit to:

  • review and respond to your enquiry;
  • understand interest in home growing, community growing, food-forest projects and nature experiences;
  • contact you about a relevant next step that relates to your enquiry;
  • maintain enquiry records, prevent spam and protect the website; and
  • meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Submitting the form does not automatically enrol you in marketing communications, confirm a booking, create a partnership or require you to make a purchase.

3. Where information is processed and stored

Website form submissions are currently processed using Google Apps Script and recorded in a Google Sheet controlled by Annavan. An enquiry notification is sent to our designated email account. Google may process information under its own terms and privacy practices. The website is hosted using Cloudflare services, which may process technical data needed to provide and secure the site.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share enquiry details with unrelated third parties for their own marketing. We may disclose information to service providers who help us operate the website or communications, where necessary to respond to your request, with your permission, or where disclosure is required by law. If a particular programme or partnership requires sharing relevant details with a site or programme partner, we will seek your permission or explain this before doing so.

5. Retention and security

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant records, manage our relationship with you and meet legal requirements. We take reasonable steps to limit access and protect records, but no website, email system or online storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to provide access to, correct or delete personal information you have submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you about an enquiry. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law and any record-retention obligations. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Children

This website's enquiry form is intended to be completed by adults. A parent, guardian or authorised adult should submit an enquiry on behalf of a child or school group and should avoid including unnecessary information about children.

8. External links and changes

This website may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We may update this policy as our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

9. Contact Annavan

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at elderjoyy@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +91 91334 14568.

This policy is intended to explain our current website enquiry practices. It is not a representation that every future Annavan activity or partner site uses the same data process; any additional collection or sharing will be explained when relevant.
